Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password. When you build a signature we store the content you enter (name, title, phone, website, etc.). When your signature is used in an email we log anonymized open and click events including a timestamp, approximate location derived from IP address, device type, and email client.

2. How We Use It

We use your account information to authenticate you and deliver the service. We use analytics event data to power your dashboard and show you engagement metrics on your signatures. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose outside of operating this service.

3. Email Communications

We send transactional emails only — account verification, password resets, and billing receipts. We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure. Passwords are hashed using Argon2id and are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by deleting your account from your Profile page or by contacting us directly.

5. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Stripe for payment processing and SendGrid for transactional email delivery. Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We share only the minimum data required for each service to function — billing information with Stripe, your email address with SendGrid.

7. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your personal data at any time through your Profile page. To request a full export of your data or to ask any privacy-related questions, contact us at the email below.

8. Contact

For any privacy concerns please reach out to privacy@signature.app.
